01 — summary

I'm a Cloud/DevOps engineer who spends most of my time turning fragile, manual infrastructure into something boring and reliable — Terraform modules instead of console clicks, Ansible playbooks instead of runbooks, pipelines instead of "deploy on Friday and hope."

My core stack is AWS and Azure, with Terraform and CloudFormation for infrastructure-as-code and Ansible for configuration automation. I build and maintain CI/CD pipelines in Azure DevOps and Jenkins, run containerized workloads on Docker Swarm and Kubernetes, and keep an eye on it all with CloudWatch, OpenSearch, and the ELK stack. I've led cloud migrations, database upgrades, and platform modernization work end-to-end — from design through production handoff.
